About Digital Voices Pakistan

Digital Voices Pakistan is a youth-led initiative designed to promote digital literacy, civic engagement, media literacy, fact-checking and responsible use of digital technologies.

Through training, storytelling, podcasts, community dialogue and digital innovation, the project empowers young people to become informed and responsible digital citizens.

Our Vision

A digitally empowered society where youth actively participate in civic life and contribute to positive social change.

Our Mission

To strengthen youth capacities through digital literacy, innovation, dialogue and community engagement.

Project Objectives

✓ Promote Digital Literacy
✓ Strengthen Civic Engagement
✓ Counter Misinformation
✓ Support Digital Storytelling
✓ Develop Youth Leadership
✓ Create Community Impact
